What I Bring to Your Big Day

Most couples think having a friend read off a script is enough. But the truth?

People remember the energy - not the programme.

As your wedding emcee, I do more than just follow the script. I bring life to every moment. I make sure your guests are engaged, entertained, and celebrating with you - not checking their phones waiting for dessert.

You're already investing so much into the day - why let it become just another wedding?

What to Expect When You Work With Me

About a month before your wedding, we'll meet to go through the flow and make sure everything runs smoothly. Here's how your celebration typically flows:

🎙️
Emcee Introduction Setting the tone with energy and warmth
💍
First March-In Spotlight on you both
🎂
Cake Cutting Sweet moments for the cameras
🍽️
Lunch/Dinner Served I keep the crowd engaged between courses
👗
Outfit Change Usually after 2nd or 3rd course
🎉
Second March-In Re-enter in style
🍾
Champagne Popping & Pouring With a bit of flair
🥂
Couple's Toast + Yam Seng Rally the crowd with energy
🎲
Games & Interactions If time permits, let's have some fun
🎤
Speeches Heartfelt, memorable, smooth
✨
Emcee Closing Ending on a high note

I handle the transitions, the energy, and the atmosphere - so you can focus on soaking in every moment.
